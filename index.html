About

Research Profile

I am a Deputy Researcher, equivalent to Associate Professor, at the Laboratory of Intelligent Collaborative Computing, UESTC. Before joining UESTC, I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from UESTC in 2022, supervised by Prof. Heng Tao Shen and co-supervised by Prof. Jie Shao. I obtained my B.S. degree in Software Engineering from UESTC in 2017.

I have independently led a series of provincial and national research projects. As principal investigator, I lead the Youth Science Fund Project (Category C) of the National Natural Science Foundation of China. I have also been awarded the Postdoctoral Science Foundation General Program (76th Batch), the Youth Project of Sichuan Provincial Natural Science Foundation, and Category B funding from the National Postdoctoral Innovative Talent Support Program.
